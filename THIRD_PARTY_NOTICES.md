## Old School RuneScape Wiki

Facts gathered from the [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/) while the data was made (never while the
plugin runs): Slayer masters and where to kill each task, some bank and banker places, clue step details, item
groups, transport networks and rune facts. Each such row names the Wiki page and revision it came from.

The Wiki's content is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).
The parts of the data derived from it are shared under that same licence, with attribution to the Wiki's
contributors.
